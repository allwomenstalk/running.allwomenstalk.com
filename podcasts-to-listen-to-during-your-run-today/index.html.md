# 10 Podcasts to Listen to during Your Run ...

Instead of listening to music or watching TV while you run, you can listen to a podcast instead. You could learn some fascinating information. According to [Self,](https://www.self.com/story/9-podcasts-that-will-make-your-next-run-100-times-better?mbid=social_twitter) you should listen to these podcasts during your next run:

## 1. Serial

![Serial](https://img.allw.mn/content/i8/n8/n38gwx2456b4b4a969253077864582_400x225.gif)

You've probably heard everyone talking about [Serial.](https://serialpodcast.org/) It's a narrative about an unsolved murder that'll keep you intrigued.

## 2. Call Your Girlfriend

_ If you want to hear about girls talk about pop culture and politics, check out Call Your Girlfriend._

## 3. Love and Radio

![footwear, leg, thigh, high heeled footwear, muscle,](https://img.allw.mn/content/ar/ix/lbqx3fk7589a217a04797865797359_474x319.gif)

[Love and Radio](http://loveandradio.org/) is all about love and sex, so you're bound to hear some interesting stories.

## 4. Start up

![structure, room, sport venue, exercise machine, gym,](https://img.allw.mn/content/r9/gs/c95l2fpe589a217a31547464303174_500x280.gif)

If you've ever thought about quitting your job to start your own business, then you should listen to [Start Up.](https://gimletmedia.com/shows/startup)

## 5. 99 Percent Invisible

![99 Percent Invisible](https://img.allw.mn/content/s5/mm/t5wkyjjn56b4b4aa6c48b639672909_450x253.gif)

[99 Percent Invisible](https://99percentinvisible.org/) is an informational podcast that'll teach you interesting facts about everyday life you can share with friends at parties.

## 6. Wait Wait Don't Tell Me

![sports, softball, tournament, soccer, soft tennis,](https://img.allw.mn/content/nx/sn/oix7gkar589a217a3230e427890165_500x270.gif)

[Wait Wait Don't Tell Me](https://www.npr.org/programs/wait-wait-dont-tell-me/?sc=embed&f=422036834?sc=embed&f=422036834) is a rare type of podcast that's also a comedic quiz show!

## 7. Happy Sad Confused

![structure, person, room, gym, sport venue,](https://img.allw.mn/content/lp/ns/cdy0r3b6589a217ce8aa9294678303_360x203.gif)

If you want to listen to celebrity interviews, you need to try listening to [Happy Sad Confused.](https://goo.gl/ESuOMO)

## 8. The Tim FerriSs Show

![person, human positions, exercise machine, product, exercise equipment,](https://img.allw.mn/content/xe/av/i48gnf1m589ae59dedb50475762504_480x270.gif)

No matter what you're in the mood for, [tim.blog](https://tim.blog/category/the-tim-ferriss-show/) has what you want. Picking the brains of experts in various fields, he finds out everything they know on their fields of expertise.

## 9. Radio Lab

![structure, room, leisure, gym, sport venue,](https://img.allw.mn/content/td/ta/pg9d7jvo589ae5ab54ce2805332669_500x272.gif)

This podcast is science meets philosophy with light-hearted tones as well as thought-provoking topics. [radiolab](https://www.radiolab.org/) is sure to keep you entertained as you tackle the miles of your run.

## 10. Another Mother RuNner

![facial expression, face, person, photography, girl,](https://img.allw.mn/content/fz/pm/bqnyjm8t589ae5e874f68372398531_500x281.gif)

If you're a mother and a runner, this is the postcast for you! [anothermotherrunner](https://anothermotherrunner.com/)
